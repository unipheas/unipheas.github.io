Brian Phillips / Autonomous CTO

Senior technical judgment, directly accountable.

01 / Accountability

The person making the recommendation stays attached to the consequence.

I work directly with founders and operators when software delivery has become expensive, opaque, or dependent on a provider the company cannot confidently evaluate.

My role is to create an evidence-backed operating view, make the hard technical decisions legible to leadership, and rebuild the parts of the system that prevent reliable delivery. I use “I” deliberately: this is senior practitioner-led work, not a sales layer in front of an anonymous delivery team.

The work is designed to leave the company stronger. That means client-controlled accounts, explicit decision records, operating documentation, enforced approval boundaries, and a handoff a qualified successor can actually use.

04 / Working style

Direct, bounded, and evidence-led.

I will tell you what I can verify.
Unknowns are recorded as unknowns. Findings carry evidence and confidence rather than borrowed certainty.
I will separate diagnosis from implementation.
The audit remains useful even if we do not work together afterward. Recommendations are not shaped to manufacture a larger engagement.
I will make tradeoffs legible.
Technical choices are translated into business consequence, timing, cost, and reversibility so the accountable person can decide.
I will preserve human authority.
Production, money, identity, data, security, legal, and destructive decisions remain behind explicit human gates.
I will build for succession.
The operating record is maintained so another qualified person can understand and continue the work.

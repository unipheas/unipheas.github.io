01 / Where it can help

Less repeated work. More useful information.

These are examples of the kinds of work we can scope together.

Company knowledge
Organize documents, project history, and operating notes so AI can use the relevant sources to help you find information and prepare work.
Meetings and follow-ups
Turn approved notes into draft summaries, proposed tasks, and follow-up messages, with review before commitments are made.
Recurring reports
Bring information from the tools you use into a recurring update, with sources and a clear indication of what needs attention.
Connected workflows
Link AI to selected business tools so an agreed task can move through preparation, checks, and approval without repeated copying and pasting.
Software development routines
Use AI to investigate issues, prepare code changes, run checks, and summarize progress within a reviewed development process.

02 / Illustrative example · Not client work

From meeting notes to a reviewed follow-up.

  1. Input

    Start with the right context.

    Provide approved meeting notes and the relevant project information. Decide which material the workflow is allowed to use.

  2. Preparation

    Draft the useful pieces.

    The workflow prepares a recap, proposed next steps, and a follow-up draft. Unclear owners or deadlines are left as questions.

  3. Review

    Check before acting.

    You confirm the facts and commitments. The agreed workflow determines what can be saved, assigned, or sent after approval.

03 / Setup and training

A workflow your team can actually use.

  1. Choose

    Find a useful first task.

    We look at how it works today, what information it needs, and how you would judge a better result. Sometimes a simpler automation is enough.

  2. Build

    Connect and test it.

    I configure the tools, instructions, and access, then test with representative work. We check output quality, review effort, failures, and running costs.

  3. Teach

    Make it part of the workday.

    I walk you through real tasks: how to ask for work, check the output, spot missing context, and handle exceptions. You get practical instructions for continuing.

04 / Ongoing use

Know what it can do. Know what needs you.

Useful AI needs the right context, a clear task, and a way to check the result.

We agree what information it can access, what actions it may take, and when a person needs to decide. I also help you understand the software subscriptions, usage costs, and maintenance the workflow needs.

The aim is a useful capability in your business, with people able to understand and oversee it.

Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the Unipheas website at unipheas.github.io and iOS apps published by Brian Phillips, including Lucky English. It explains what information these products collect and how outside services may process technical request data.

This website

This website does not use accounts, a contact form, advertising trackers, first-party analytics, cookies, or browser storage. Contact links open your email application; any message you choose to send is handled by your email provider and mine. If you arrive through a campaign link, non-personal campaign labels may stay in the page URL and be included in the email draft for you to review. They are not saved in browser storage or sent to an analytics service by this site.

Website infrastructure

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Its typefaces and other site assets are served from the same website. GitHub may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested URL under its own privacy policy. I do not receive a profile of your browsing activity from GitHub Pages.

Published apps

The apps covered by this policy do not ask users to create accounts and do not intentionally collect personal data, behavioral analytics, advertising identifiers, or precise location. Apple may process purchase, download, crash, or device information as part of operating the App Store and its platform services under Apple’s policies.

Changes

This policy may change if the website, an existing app, or a new app begins using different services. Material changes will be published here with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to brian.phillips@unipheas.com.

Privacy policy.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Scope

This policy covers unipheas.com and iOS apps published by Brian Phillips, including Lucky English. It explains what information these products collect and how outside services may process technical request data.

This website

Unipheas.com does not use an account system, contact form, advertising tracker, or first-party analytics. The site stores only your selected color theme and language preference in your browser. Contact links open your email application; any message you choose to send is handled by your email provider and mine.

Website infrastructure

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address, browser details, and requested URL under their own privacy policies. I do not receive a profile of your browsing activity from those services.

Published apps

The apps covered by this policy do not ask users to create accounts and do not intentionally collect personal data, behavioral analytics, advertising identifiers, or precise location. Apple may process purchase, download, crash, or device information as part of operating the App Store and its platform services under Apple’s policies.

Changes

This policy may change if the website, an existing app, or a new app begins using different services. Material changes will be published here with a revised date.

Contact

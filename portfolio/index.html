Let’s Ride

Rebuild the platform.
Give the business a way to keep building.

My roleTechnical Consultant

Previously Fractional CTO

Scope

Mobile, API, web, AWS infrastructure, AI development systems, and operations.

Let’s Ride is an off-road navigation and community product. I took over technical delivery from an outsourced vendor, rebuilt the platform and its AWS foundation, and built CADE from the ground up. The work connected product engineering with a development and release process the owner could use.

The platform rebuild

I worked across the React Native mobile apps, Laravel API, and web applications. I implemented Apple and Google sign-in, rotating refresh tokens, and cross-platform session management, and deployed self-hosted Valhalla routing for route planning and navigation.

I led the mobile store launch work, including account access, Sign in with Apple, age verification, and user-generated content moderation requirements. The scope covered the product and the services it depended on.

AWS & delivery infrastructure

I rebuilt production infrastructure on AWS with Terraform, ECS, RDS, CloudFront, and WAF. I established CI/CD, monitoring, backup validation, and incident-response processes alongside the infrastructure.

That work included operating the routing service on ECS Fargate, connecting infrastructure alerts to the engineering workflow, and checking application behavior after releases.

CADE: from incident to engineering work

I built CADE to connect AI-assisted development to the running product. Sentry incidents feed agent work and pull requests; GitHub, automated builds and tests, and AWS tooling connect implementation to delivery and operations.

The system gives agents access to the project context and tools needed to investigate issues, prepare changes, and submit them for review. It supports both product changes and ongoing maintenance.

Put the owner in the development loop

I enabled the owner to develop app changes using AI and simulator feedback: describe the change, inspect the result, test it, and move it through the build and release workflow.

I also taught the owner how to use the toolchain, review proposed work, check system state, and participate in deployments. The goal was a business that could continue developing its product with the code, tools, and operating knowledge in hand.

Release controls & ongoing operations

Builds and automated tests run with code changes. Staging deployments are automated; production releases always require approval. Release work includes preflight checks, deployment verification, and smoke testing.

I established practices for backend-before-mobile release sequencing, feature controls, and rollback, and continue as a Technical Consultant on the platform and its operations.

This is the work behind my consulting practice: hands-on engineering, a repeatable delivery process, and the knowledge a business needs to operate what it owns.

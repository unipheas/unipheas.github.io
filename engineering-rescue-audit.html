An independent review of how your product is built, released, and operated. Useful when ownership is unclear, changes are difficult, or you need a grounded plan before improving the development process.

Let’s talk ↗

01 / What we review

The product and the operation behind it.

Ownership and access
Who controls the code, hosting, services, and release accounts, and what the business can access directly.
Development and releases
How changes are prepared, checked, and published. Whether another person can follow the process.
Reliability and maintenance
Recurring problems, monitoring, dependencies, and the work needed to keep the product operating.
Knowledge and handoffs
Where important decisions and operating instructions live, and what is missing when people change.

02 / What you receive

A clear picture and a practical next step.

Findings that separate verified problems from open questions, with the implications for your business.

The agreed deliverables can include an ownership map, a review of the release process, and prioritized recommendations for stabilizing or improving the operation.

We set the scope, fee, and timing before the review starts. Implementation is scoped separately.

03 / How the review works

Agree the questions. Examine the evidence.

We identify the decisions the review needs to support and the access required. The review is read-only unless we explicitly agree implementation work.

I walk you through the findings, their limits, and the recommended priorities. You can use the assessment to guide your own team or discuss a follow-on engagement.

An audit is optional. A focused workflow or release problem may have a smaller, more useful starting point.

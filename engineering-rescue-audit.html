Common audit questions.

Will you change our production systems?

The audit is read-only by default. Production changes and remediation are separate work, authorized through an agreed implementation scope.

What access will you need?

Typically read access to relevant repositories, deployment configuration, monitoring, operating documentation, account inventories, and available vendor and cost records. The exact list is agreed for your system.

Do we have to hire you afterward?

No. The audit is a standalone engagement. Your team can use the findings and roadmap independently.

Is this an AI readiness assessment?

The audit examines the underlying software operation. Where relevant, it identifies prerequisites for AI development workflows; it is not a blanket certification of AI readiness or security compliance.

Can we start with a smaller question?

Yes. A bounded architecture or delivery question can be scoped at the standard $350 USD hourly rate. We agree the expected effort before starting.

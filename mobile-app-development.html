02 / Knowledge that stays with you

Make a release someone else can repeat.

A mobile product needs company-controlled access and a process that survives changes in personnel.

I help organize account ownership, build instructions, testing steps, and release procedures. We exercise the process on agreed work, find gaps, and train the people who will use it.

Store review is an external step in that process. The system should make preparation, review follow-up, and release coordination understandable.

More than 15 years across mobile, full-stack development, and software systems informs how I approach the work.

I can follow a product problem from the experience on a device through the services and infrastructure supporting it. That helps connect the development process to the actual system it needs to maintain.

iOS & Android development

From the first idea
to the app people use.

Mobile development is my specialty. I help define the product, design how it works, build the app and its supporting systems, and take it through testing and release.

Let’s talk ↗

01 / Plan and design

Work out what the app needs to do.

We start with the people using it and the problem it should solve.

I help turn the concept into user flows, a practical feature scope, and a technical plan. A prototype can help us test the experience before committing to the full build.

For an existing app, I review what is already there and help decide what to keep, improve, or rebuild.

02 / Development

The app and the systems behind it.

iOS and Android
Native iOS development and cross-platform apps with React Native. We choose an approach based on the product, device requirements, budget, and maintenance needs.
Backend and integrations
The accounts, APIs, data, and third-party services that support the experience in the app.
Web and internal tools
Admin dashboards, web experiences, and tools your team needs to support the product.
Existing app improvements
New features, bug fixes, development handoffs, and updates to keep the app and its dependencies current.

03 / Testing and release

A working prototype is one step along the way.

Getting ready for customers includes testing the app, preparing its supporting systems, and coordinating the release.

  1. Test

    Check real user journeys.

    Device testing, beta builds, and checks of the important flows: signing in, moving through screens, and using the features your product depends on.

  2. Prepare

    Coordinate the moving parts.

    Make sure the backend and app work together, prepare store information, and agree how the release will be reviewed and rolled out.

  3. Release

    Support the store process.

    App Store and Google Play submission support, review follow-up, and release coordination. Store review remains subject to each platform’s requirements.

  4. Improve

    Stay involved after launch.

    Monitor errors and feedback, prioritize fixes, and plan subsequent updates. Ongoing support is scoped around what your product needs.

04 / How I work

AI in the process. Engineering experience throughout.

I use AI to support research, implementation, testing, documentation, and routine development work.

I remain responsible for the technical choices and the work I deliver. Changes still need review and testing against what the product is supposed to do.

You can work with me on a new app, an existing product, or a specific part of the development process.

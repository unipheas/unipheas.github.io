Fractional CTO

Executive technical leadership, without a full-time hire.

You don't always need a permanent CTO. You need someone who can own the hard technical decisions, hold the team and vendors accountable, and be answerable for whether the product actually ships.

I operate as the senior technical leader in the room — setting direction, making the architecture and build-vs-buy calls, running planning, and giving leadership a clear, honest read on where engineering really stands. I go as deep into the work as the situation demands, then leave the team stronger than I found it.

What I Solve

Most teams call me when something is already on fire.

Delivery has stalled

Deadlines keep slipping, nobody can explain why, and confidence inside and outside the team is gone. I find the real bottleneck and get delivery moving again.

The vendor isn't delivering

You're paying an agency or offshore team and losing control of quality, timelines, and code you can't see into. I step in as your technical authority and hold them accountable.

The product is unstable

Releases break, the architecture fights the team, and every change feels risky. I stabilize the system and make shipping safe and routine again.

No senior technical voice

Decisions stall because no one owns the technical call. I provide the experienced judgment your team and your board are looking for.

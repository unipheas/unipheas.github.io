How I Work

AI-augmented engineering.

I don't just use AI tools — I architect how teams work with them.

I've developed what I call CADE — Continuous Autonomous Development Environment. It's a governed automation platform where tickets flow through AI-powered planning, implementation, code review, and testing agents, with human approval gates at every critical step. Think of it as CI/CD for the entire development workflow, not just the deploy.

The toolchain includes Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, n8n workflow automation, custom MCP servers, and purpose-built AI skills. I'm not experimenting with this — I'm shipping production software with it. Infinite Miner, a live browser game with procedural generation and prestige systems, was built entirely with AI-augmented development.

This isn't about replacing engineers. It's about making a team of 3 ship like a team of 10.

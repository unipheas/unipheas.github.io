FAQ

The questions behind the first call.

Is this a development agency?

No. I work on the client side of the table. The objective is to create technical ownership, reliable delivery, and an operation the company controls—not a permanent dependency on my labor.

Do I have to buy all three stages?

No. The audit stands on its own and leaves you with a usable risk assessment and roadmap. The Foundation Build or Autonomous Operations only proceeds when the evidence supports it.

Will agents deploy code without approval?

No. The system can automate low-risk work, but production promotions, app-store releases, payments, authentication, migrations, destructive data changes, legal-adjacent work, and spending increases remain behind human gates.

What do we own when the engagement ends?

The operating system installed for your company: infrastructure, workflows, routines, technical memory, and handoff documentation. Accounts and payment methods remain under client control throughout.

Who is the right fit?

A founder or operator with a real software business, meaningful revenue or funding, and an expensive delivery problem. The strongest fit is a company paying agency-level costs while lacking visibility, reliability, or internal technical ownership.

A serious first conversation

Tell me what leadership cannot verify.

Describe the product, the current team or vendor structure, the approximate monthly delivery cost, and the question you cannot get answered clearly. I’ll tell you directly whether an Engineering Rescue Audit is a sensible next step.

Start with the operating facts.

A useful first note includes:

  • Product or company and what it supports
  • Current internal team and outside providers
  • Approximate monthly delivery run-rate
  • What is stalled, risky, or unclear
  • The business event making this urgent

Before you write

A quick fit check.

Worth comparing notes

  • A real product with revenue, funding, or material operating dependence
  • Agency-level spend or comparable business exposure
  • Missed delivery, unclear ownership, or fragile releases
  • A decision-maker prepared to provide evidence and access

Likely a different need

  • Idea-stage product design or speculative app development
  • A request to confirm a predetermined vendor dispute conclusion
  • Guaranteed launch, savings, security, or recovery outcomes
  • Automation without testing, rollback, or accountable human approval

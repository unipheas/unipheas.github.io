Build the process.

We start with what your business needs to accomplish and how product work happens today. I put the agreed tools and processes in place, work through real tasks, and teach you or your team how to continue.

01 / Agree the first step

A clear scope and a useful outcome.

You may have an idea to bring to market, a team that needs a better way to work, or an existing product that needs to stabilize.

We identify the most useful starting point and what access and information it requires. Before paid work begins, you receive the scope, fee, timing, and how we will judge completion.

The first engagement might establish a release process, introduce an AI workflow, or assess an existing operation. Broader work is agreed as we learn more.

02 / How the engagement works

From understanding to a working system.

  1. Understand

    Map the current operation.

    Look at product priorities, people, code, accounts, knowledge, and how a task reaches production. Identify the gaps that matter to the business.

  2. Establish

    Connect the tools and the process.

    Configure the agreed development environment, AI workflows, review steps, testing, and release procedures. Document the decisions and access.

  3. Exercise

    Use it on real work.

    Take a defined task through the process. Check the output and the handoffs, investigate failures, and refine the instructions.

  4. Teach

    Make the knowledge usable.

    Walk you or your team through directing work, checking results, making releases, and handling exceptions.

  5. Sustain

    Agree how it will keep running.

    Define ongoing ownership, maintenance tasks, and when specialist support is needed. Scope continued guidance separately.

03 / What stays with your business

Working tools and the knowledge to use them.

Company-controlled access
The relevant code, accounts, and documentation belong under your business’s control. We identify gaps and arrange the access required.
A documented process
Instructions for setup, review, testing, releases, and common operating tasks, matched to the engagement.
Trained people
Practical walkthroughs for the owners or team members who will direct and oversee the work.
Clear technical responsibility
You work directly with me on recommendations and implementation. I explain what is verified, what remains uncertain, and which decisions need your input.

04 / Common questions

How this fits your business.

Do I need an existing development team?

No. We can establish a process around the people and resources you have. We agree who will direct work, review technical changes, and operate the product; some responsibilities still require engineering expertise.

Can you help before the product exists?

Yes. We can define the product priorities and establish the development tools, knowledge, and process needed to move from concept toward a first release.

Do you work on the product itself?

Yes, where implementation is needed to establish or validate the development system. The engagement is scoped around building a lasting capability in your business, including the practical work needed to make it usable.

Can you work with my current developers?

Yes. I can work alongside your team, help with a handoff, and improve how knowledge, technical decisions, and releases are managed.

Do I need the full audit before you can help?

No. We choose a starting point based on the work. A software audit is useful when we need a broader picture of an existing system.

Can you stay involved?

Yes. We can agree ongoing technical guidance, workflow maintenance, and improvements as your product and business change.

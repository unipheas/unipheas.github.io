01 / The first conversation

Define what success looks like.

Tell me what you want to create or improve, who it is for, and what is making it important now.

We work through the options, assumptions, and constraints. The first engagement might be a product plan, a prototype, an AI workflow, a focused repair, or a broader build.

I set out the scope, fee, expected timing, and access needed before paid work begins. If we uncover a larger problem or a new opportunity, we agree changes before expanding the work.

02 / The development process

From an idea to something you can rely on.

  1. Plan

    Define the product.

    Clarify the users, business goal, essential features, and what a useful first version should include.

  2. Design

    Work through the experience.

    Develop user flows, screen concepts, and prototypes. Make technical choices with the build and future maintenance in mind.

  3. Build

    Implement in reviewable steps.

    Develop the app, web experience, backend, integrations, or AI workflow. Review progress and adjust priorities together.

  4. Test

    Check the intended behavior.

    Test the important user journeys and system interactions. For AI work, check representative inputs, output quality, and exception handling.

  5. Release

    Prepare for real use.

    Coordinate the deployment or app-store release, check the live result, and document the steps for operating it.

  6. Improve

    Learn from use.

    Review errors, feedback, and changing needs. Scope maintenance, further development, or ongoing guidance as appropriate.

03 / Working with your team

Keep the knowledge in your business.

Direct collaboration
You work with me on both the recommendations and the implementation. I can also work alongside your developers or agency.
Practical training
I explain how the tools and workflows fit together, and walk through the tasks you or your team will handle.
Company access
Your company should control its code, accounts, and relevant documentation. I identify gaps and help arrange the access needed.
Useful handoff
I document setup, release steps, operating procedures, and important decisions so someone else can continue the work.

04 / AI and automation

Use the tools. Check the work.

AI is part of my engineering practice and a capability I can help you introduce into your own business.

We choose what to automate based on the task, the available information, and how the output can be checked. I review the work I deliver and explain the decisions that need your input.

Before changes reach customers or affect sensitive data, access, or spending, we agree the checks and appropriate approval.

05 / Common questions

Where should we start?

Can you help if I only have an idea?

Yes. We can work through the problem, audience, user experience, and practical scope before deciding what to build.

Can you help with AI without building a new app?

Yes. We can focus on an existing business process, connected knowledge, recurring work, or training with the tools you already use.

Can you work with my current developer or agency?

Yes. I can provide technical guidance, coordinate work, or handle an agreed part of development.

Do I need the full audit before you can help?

No. We choose a first engagement based on the problem. The Engineering Rescue Audit is available when a broader independent review is useful. You can also read a fictional handoff assessment to see the format.

Can you stay involved after launch?

Yes. We can agree ongoing development, maintenance, AI workflow improvements, or technical guidance separately.

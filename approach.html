How the work gets done.

  1. Understand

    Establish the starting point.

    Review the product, technical constraints, and current delivery process. Identify the decisions and outcomes that matter.

  2. Agree

    Put the engagement in writing.

    Define deliverables, access, responsibilities, acceptance criteria, and commercial terms. Identify dependencies on your team.

  3. Implement

    Work through real changes.

    Build and validate the agreed system against representative product work. Report what is complete, what is blocked, and which decisions need input.

  4. Handoff

    Leave the operation understandable.

    Deliver code, configuration, runbooks, and a walkthrough with the people responsible for continued use. Agree any ongoing work separately.

A few practical questions.

Who is this work for?

Companies with an active software product or platform, a technical sponsor, and a defined reason to invest in delivery improvements. This includes enterprise teams and startups building their engineering capacity.

Do we need the audit first?

No. If the problem and starting point are clear, we can scope implementation or hourly consulting directly.

Can you work alongside our engineers or existing vendor?

Yes. Responsibilities, review authority, and communication are agreed with the people already involved.

Does the monthly partnership include 24/7 support?

No. Reserved capacity, response expectations, and operating responsibilities are written into the scope. Around-the-clock coverage and unlimited development are not included by default.

How do we evaluate progress?

Agree a baseline and acceptance criteria for the engagement. Depending on the work, these may include release lead time, failure rate, review effort, operating cost, or the ability of another engineer to run the system.
